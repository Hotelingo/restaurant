How to review in one pass

  1. Choose what to see. “Show” at the top: R1 is what pilot restaurants get, R1–R2 adds item level and forecast, All adds scale features.
  2. Walk the journey in the left list, top to bottom. It follows the monthly loop below.
  3. On each screen use the panel on the right: what changed against your draft, what to check in the guides, the decisions for you, then mark Accept, Needs change or Question.
  4. Try the controls. “View as” switches role; several screens refuse bad input the way the product must (mapping, food bridge, decisions, sign-off).
  5. Come back here and copy the summary below.
